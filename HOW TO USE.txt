NEUROLOGY OPD REGISTER
Movement disorders and epilepsy
No coding needed.

OPEN IT
1. Double-click "Open the app.bat".
2. A black window stays open. Leave it open.
3. Chrome opens the register. Always use the same browser.
4. Do not use Incognito / private mode, or the records can disappear when you close it.

ON YOUR PHONE
1. Connect the phone to the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
2. In the black window, find a line like http://192.168.x.x:8787/
3. Type that address into Chrome on the phone.
4. In Chrome: menu, then "Add to Home screen".
5. If Windows asks about a firewall, allow it on private networks.
Voice works best in Google Chrome.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Press "Record patient details" once and say the name, age, sex, OPD number, phone, and diagnosis in one sentence.
- Or tap New patient and type. Small Speak buttons fill one box at a time.
- The phone number stays on the patient card. Tap it to call.
- Search box: type a phone number or a diagnosis, such as 9876543210, Parkinson, stroke, or migraine.
- Other neurology is the place for stroke, headache, neuropathy, and any diagnosis that is not a movement disorder or epilepsy.
- Filters: Movement, Epilepsy, Other neurology, Follow-up due, Today.
- Add visit: copies the last diagnosis and medicines so follow-up is faster.

VOICE
Press "Record patient details" once, then say:
name Maharani Das, age 24, sex female, OPD number Jajpur 21, phone 9876543210, diagnosis parkinsonism

It stops by itself when you finish speaking. Check the boxes, then tap Save.

You can also tap "Tap and speak" and say one thing:

- Phone 98765 43210
- Age 54
- Female
- Diagnosis essential tremor
- Medicine propranolol 40 mg morning and night
- Search stroke
- Search 9000000001
- Save

Or tap the small Speak button on a box and talk only into that box.
In Settings you can switch the voice language to Hindi.

TRY IT ONCE
1. Open Settings and tap "Add practice patients".
2. In the search box, type Parkinson. Then try the phone number 9000000001.
3. Tap Other neurology to see a stroke practice record.
4. Delete the practice patients from Settings when you want a clean register.
Practice names start with EXAMPLE. The phone numbers are 90000 00001 and similar, so they are not real.

KEEP YOUR RECORDS SAFE
- Settings, then "Download backup", once a week.
- Keep that file somewhere safe (a USB drive or email to yourself).
- "Restore a backup" brings the records back onto a browser.
- "Download phone list" makes a simple text file of names, phones, and diagnoses.
- Records stay on this device. This app does not upload them.
- This app only stores your notes. It does not give medical advice or doses.

PUT YOUR CLINIC NAME
Settings: type the clinic name and your name. They appear on the printed visit note.
